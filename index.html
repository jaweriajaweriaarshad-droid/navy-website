OUR MISSION

About Pakistan Navy

The Pakistan Navy is the naval branch of Pakistan's armed forces. Its responsibilities include protecting maritime interests, safeguarding sea routes and contributing to maritime security.

This educational website explores naval heritage, maritime service, naval technology and career information.
